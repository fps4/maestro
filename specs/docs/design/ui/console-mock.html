Eleven screens for a service whose whole product is that a record cannot change afterwards

The console for fps4/mstr-specs — the specification service realising PS3 of the maestro platform. Every screen below is built from the real seeded workspace (ws-aannemer-x: opportunity → business case → specification → intake assessment → pack binding), the real facet names from PS3 §4, real standards from the NL-construction model, and the real gates from the workspace definition. Nothing here is placeholder text.

New in v3 — the standards live here too. A standard has a lineage, immutable versions, a publication gate with an accountable human, a diff, a body someone reads and facets a machine evaluates. That is this service's shape item for item, so the catalogue is not a second system — it is this one with a different workspace definition. Ours carry full text and the know-how an agent needs; external ones carry a citation, our summary and our reading, because ISO's licence forbids more. The binding records which of them this tenant accepted, who accepted each, and when.

So specification here means a governed statement of what must be true — which a business case, a specification and a standard all are. The name stays; the definition is the one in GLOSSARY.md.

The design decision under every screen: a draft and a version must never look like the same object. Drafts carry dashed edges and live controls; versions carry a solid rule, a monospace digest and no edit affordance at all. That is ADR-0003 rendered rather than described.

Full text — ours, so the body is the standard

markdown/v1 · rendered
## The obligation A business case put to the Explore gate SHALL carry a declared_outcome with a baseline, a target, and a unit, where the baseline is measured and the measurement method is stated. ## Why we hold this An estimated baseline cannot be falsified, so no later conformance assessment can say whether the outcome was achieved. Every business case we declined for insufficiency in the first year failed here, and in eleven of fourteen the sponsor believed they had a number. ## What counts as measured - Observed over at least five instances of the process - Collected before the case was written, not for it - Reproducible by someone other than the author An estimate is admissible only with consequence class c1 or c2 and an explicit `baseline_estimated: true`, which downgrades every conformance claim built on it. ## How to satisfy it quickly Most werkvoorbereiding processes already leave a trace in a planning tool. Six projects of history is usually one export away, and that is the cheapest measurement available — see the elicitation construct `ct-baseline-probe@4`.

This is the body a person reads and an agent retrieves. The gate does not read it — it reads the assertion facet below.
